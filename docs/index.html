Frequently Asked Questions

Does Rhythm collect any user data? +

No, Rhythm operates entirely offline with zero trackers, ads, or internet requirements. Your music data stays completely private on your device.

How does the Dynamic Theming work? +

Rhythm extracts core color tones dynamically from your active song's album art, morphing the application interfaces fluidly to match the visual vibe of the track.

What does the Library Janitor feature do? +

The Library Janitor helps clean up your local audio library by scanning and scrubbing duplicate tracks, fixing messy metadata tags, and fetching missing album art profiles.

How do I create ringtones or notification clips? +

You can use the built-in Precision Trimmer to select any song from your library, trim your favorite hook down to the exact millisecond, and save it as a system audio clip instantly.

What is Driving Mode? +

Driving Mode introduces a simplified, distraction-free playback interface with large tactile controls and high-contrast styling layouts optimized for safe road use.
